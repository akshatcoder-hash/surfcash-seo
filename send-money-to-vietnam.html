Best way to send money to Vietnam in 2026

There isn't one best corridor. There is a best match for how you hold money, and whether the person on the other end has a Vietnamese bank.

What the recipient actually needs

Almost every real payment in Vietnam ends as dong in a Vietnamese bank account. Landlords, family, cleaners, motorbike shops, and street vendors settle on VietQR or a local transfer. They do not want a SWIFT reference, a Western Union queue number, or a wallet they have never opened.

So the job is not "move dollars across a border." The job is "land VND in their bank, with a receipt, without you opening a Vietnamese account." VietQR is the rail. Everything else is a way of feeding that rail.

The four situations people mix up

These get one article and four different answers:

You live here, income arrives in USD or crypto

You need rent, deposits, groceries, and a motorbike paid in VND every month. This is a local-pay problem, not a remittance problem.

Someone abroad is paying a person in Vietnam

A parent, a client, a co-founder. The receiver has a bank. Speed and a clean receipt matter more than the brand on the app.

You only have a US or EU bank, no crypto

A bank-to-bank app (Wise and similar) is still the straightforward path. Do not buy crypto just to scan a QR once.

The receiver can only pick up cash

Use a cash-pickup network. A VietQR scanner cannot help a person with no bank.

How the usual options actually behave

Wise / bank wire. Works from a normal bank account. Recipient needs account details. One to three days is typical. Existing public quotes sit around 2.5% plus whatever your bank stacks on top. Fine for a one-off. A grind if you pay rent this way every month.

ATM cash. It works until it doesn't. Foreign cards hit withdrawal caps, $5–$7 machine fees, and you walk around with a month of rent in your bag. Landlords will take it. They will not have a useful record when the deposit fight starts.

Informal "money guys." Still common on Telegram. Fast, expensive, no receipt, and you are trusting a stranger with the month's money.

Crypto P2P. USDT to VND via a local bank is a real corridor. It is also where people get scammed or frozen. Last resort, not a rent workflow.

Scan the recipient's VietQR from a crypto balance. If you already hold USDC or crypto, this is the direct path: they show the same QR their bank app already has, you scan, they see dong. They never open a new app. SurfCash does this. So do other QR-pay apps — Moreta charges about 1.5% per payment; LocalPay is spread-based with no flat fee published.

When scanning a VietQR is the better send

Use it when all three are true: you already hold digital dollars or crypto, the recipient has a Vietnamese bank, and you want the money there in seconds with a record. That is most foreigners paying rent, a deposit, or a person they already know.

1. Hold the balance

Import the wallet you already use, or buy USDC in-app. SurfCash is non-custodial — keys stay on your device.

2. Get their VietQR

Every Vietnamese banking app can show one. Rent invoices often print it. Family can screenshot it.

3. Scan, check the amount in VND, send

The app converts and settles to their bank. They see a normal incoming transfer. You see a receipt.

If the send is rent

The most common "send money to Vietnam" search from people who already live here is rent. Two-month deposits, a printed VietQR on the contract, a landlord who will not take Visa. That walkthrough is separate: how to pay rent in Vietnam without a bank account.

If you do not live here and you are only moving salary home to family with a bank, a remittance app from your home bank is still a reasonable default. If you already hold the balance as digital dollars, skip the wire and pay their VietQR.
