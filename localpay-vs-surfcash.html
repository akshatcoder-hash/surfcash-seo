Where LocalPay is strong

LocalPay is a sharp, focused product: scan a VietQR with USDT on Solana, done in a couple seconds. It's non-custodial, so you keep your keys. If you're a crypto-native traveler who wants the simplest possible QR tap in Vietnam, it does that job cleanly.

Where SurfCash goes further

1. Yield, not idle

Both are non-custodial. But SurfCash puts your balance to work — up to 15% via Perena, with a fixed, insured USD* Protected option. Your deposit earns while it's parked.

2. Built for the month, not the tap

Recurring rent, two-month deposits, and a landlord who gets dong straight into their bank account. That's the SurfCash core loop.

3. One app beyond Vietnam

VietQR, PromptPay and PIX — Vietnam, Thailand and Brazil in one wallet. LocalPay is Vietnam-only today.

4. eSIM + borrow

Land, install an eSIM, borrow against your crypto if you're short dong on a Friday. More reasons to hold the balance with SurfCash.

Which should you pick?

Pick LocalPay if you want the leanest possible QR tap and you're Vietnam-only for now.

Pick SurfCash if you want the same tap plus yield on your balance, rent/deposit rails, multiple countries, and eSIM/borrow in the same app.
