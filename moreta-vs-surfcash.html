Where Moreta is strong

Moreta has been around longer and covers more countries — useful if you're hopping around Southeast Asia or beyond and want one app for many corridors. It's a polished, custodial experience: top up USD, pay QRs. If you're a short-stay tourist who just wants a coffee paid, it works.

Where SurfCash is different

1. It's for people who stay, not just visit

Recurring rent, two-month deposits, and a loyalty score that rewards locals — SurfCash is built for the 12-month lease, not the 3-day trip.

2. Your money stays productive

In a custodial wallet, your balance sits at 0%. SurfCash is non-custodial and earns yield while it waits — up to 15% via Perena, or a fixed, insured USD* Protected option.

3. Lower fee

0.5% versus ~1.5% per payment. On a year of monthly rent, that difference compounds.

4. You keep the keys

Non-custodial means SurfCash can't freeze or touch your funds. Your crypto is on-chain, on your device.

Which should you pick?

Pick Moreta if you're a short-stay tourist bouncing between 5+ countries and just want QR taps on the go.

Pick SurfCash if you're living in Vietnam (or Thailand/Brazil), paying rent and deposits every month, and want your balance earning instead of sitting dead — at a lower fee, with your own keys.
