Why paying in Vietnam is hard as a foreigner

Vietnam runs on VietQR — a bank-to-bank QR network. Your Visa card works at Starbucks and not much else. It can't pay your landlord, your grab of groceries at the market, or your motorbike deposit.

Cards don't work at ~70% of merchants. Street vendors, markets, landlords and small shops only take cash or VietQR. ATMs cap your withdrawals and charge $5–$7 a pop.
You can't open a local account easily. No visa, no work permit, no local bank. So you carry cash and hope nothing goes wrong.

How SurfCash fixes it

1. Hold digital dollars

Import your existing wallet or buy USDC in-app. Your keys stay on your device — non-custodial.

2. Scan any VietQR

Point at the merchant's or landlord's QR. SurfCash converts and settles local currency in ~10 seconds.

3. They just see dong

Your landlord gets VND in their banking app. They never need to know what crypto or Solana is.
